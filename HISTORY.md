# Earlier proofs

The dated drafts below record how the arguments developed. The two current preprints are linked from the [front page](README.md). File fingerprints for the archived drafts are in [SHA256SUMS](historical-drafts/SHA256SUMS).

### 22 November 2026 · Classical small powers

- **Fourth powers.** Two drafts recover Davenport’s value of 16. One follows Davenport and Erdős through diminishing ranges and a density argument. The other uses translation, sampling, and a smooth cutoff to bring the quartic Vinogradov mean value theorem to bear. [Classical proof](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/Waring_G4_Compact_Classical_Proof.pdf) · [TeX](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/quartic_classical_density.tex) · [Modern proof](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/Waring_G4_Modern_Weighted_Proof.pdf) · [TeX](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/quartic_weighted_short.tex)
- **Seventh powers.** Six ordinary roots and twenty-five smooth roots recover Wooley’s bound of 31. Section 7 of the current classical paper gives the short calculation. The ensuing investigation improved auxiliary estimates and measured the gap at 30 variables. [Follow-up working draft](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/G7_Three_Hour_Investigation.pdf) · [TeX](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/G7_Three_Hour_Investigation_Source.tex)
- **Eighth and ninth powers.** The working manuscript gives efficient-differencing arguments for 38 and 46 summands, respectively. Its PDF records a compilation time of **22:12:29 UTC** (17:12:29 EST). Chow and Dietmann’s arXiv v1 submission of *A linear bound in Waring’s problem* is timestamped **22:55:51 UTC** the same Sunday, 43 minutes and 22 seconds later. The first time is the local compilation recorded in the PDF. The second is arXiv’s receipt of their submission. The current classical paper uses their factors-and-fibres construction to give another proof for 38 eighth powers and to reach 44 ninth powers. [Working PDF](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/Gk_Target_Selection_Working_Manuscript.pdf) · [TeX preserved with the Lean checkpoint](historical-drafts/2026-11-22/Gk_Target_Selection_Working_Manuscript.tex) · [Chow and Dietmann, v1 submission history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29821v1)

### 24 November 2026 · Transfers to number fields

A research note dated 24 November gives the bounds 17, 24, 31 and 38 for fifth through eighth powers over arbitrary number fields. The TeX source carries the 24 November date. The preserved PDF records a recompilation on 27 November. The small-powers supplement develops the smooth-moment and local estimates through degree ten. Section 8 of the main paper joins them to the fibre argument for ninth and tenth powers. Two other earlier arguments retain their own uses. Section 3 develops efficient differencing for arbitrary ideal classes, and Section 9 counts eight-cube representations by combining two four-cube sums before giving the seven-cube construction.
